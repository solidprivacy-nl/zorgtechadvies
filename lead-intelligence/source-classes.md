# Lead Intelligence Source Classes

## A — Leadership change

Examples: CIO, manager ICT, manager digitale zorg, bestuurder, informatiemanager zorgtechnologie.

**Use:** strong timing signal because mandate/strategy may still be open.

## B — Tender, market consultation, prior information notice

Sources: TenderNed, Mercell, TED and relevant procurement portals.

**Use:** very high timing value when still pre-decision.

**Priority:** operationalise first.

## C — Real estate / new build / renovation

New care locations and major renovations can force VOS, domotics, access, infrastructure and monitoring decisions 12–24 months earlier.

## D — Lifecycle / replacement horizon

Use historical livegang, procurement and supplier-case evidence to estimate possible reorientation windows.

**Important:** lifecycle is an indicator, not proof of a replacement date. Do not hard-code a universal 5–8 year replacement law.

## E — Supervision / incident / Wzd

Sources may include public IGJ findings and other authoritative public material.

**Use:** organisational risk signal and research input.

**Ethical restriction:** not an automatic direct-sales trigger. Apply the outreach ethical gate in `model-v3.2.md`.

## F — Supplier cases

Useful both as evidence of recent selection and as "negative space" for lifecycle research.

Supplier claims remain supplier claims until independently verified.

## G — Annual report / quality report / board report

Supporting evidence only. Often lagging 6–18 months; do not use as the sole timing trigger when avoidable.

## H — Advisory-firm publications / incumbent adviser

**Corrected v3.2 rule:** context signal, not a gate.

Presence of another adviser can mean:

- the need is already covered;
- the need has moved to a new phase;
- independent assurance or second opinion is relevant;
- a different domain remains open.

Verify rather than auto-disqualify.

## Source quality rule

For every material fact store:

- source URL/reference;
- publication or observed date;
- source class;
- what exactly the source supports;
- whether it is independent or self-serving;
- verification status.

Multiple URLs are not automatically multiple independent sources.
