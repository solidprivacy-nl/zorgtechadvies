## Hero

Eyebrow: `Vendor-neutraal advies over zorgtechnologie`

Primary headline: `Zorgtechnologie vernieuwen? Krijg eerst scherp wat er écht nodig is.`

Core promise: the KetenScan aligns care, ICT, real estate and governance around the real assignment and the first defensible step before investing in products or trajectories.

Primary CTA: exploratory conversation.

## KetenScan proof

Live site currently communicates:

- diagnosis of the real core problem;
- three scenarios with choices, risks and trade-offs;
- defensible decision memo;
- chain check covering governance, infrastructure, privacy/security, operations and adoption;
- live displayed duration: 3–6 weeks;
- live displayed price: €8,500–€14,500 excl. VAT;
- no mandatory follow-up.

## Problem framing

Three current patterns:

1. no clear chain owner;
2. demos/product choice before diagnosis;
3. pilots without decision moments.

## KetenScan flow

1. Diagnosis — core assignment.
2. Evaluation — scenarios and trade-offs.
3. Decision — advice and first executable step toward PvE, selection, PoV or project reset.

## Current situations/cases

- stalled VOS trajectory;
- supplier choice before decision;
- smart sensors/video AI requiring better framing.

## Team/expertise layer

The live site already shows broader capability beyond the KetenScan, including:

- programme/project direction;
- infrastructure and care-critical connectivity;
- smart sensors, cameras and AI;
- privacy/security/governance;
- adoption/workability/assurance;
- PvE, supplier selection and Proof of Value.

This supports extending the site without changing the hero into a full-portfolio catalogue.

## Contact

Current CTA remains a short exploratory online conversation, with email/phone and Calendly alternatives.

## Baseline implication

The current site is strategically closer to the updated model than earlier HTML versions: it already presents wider expertise while keeping one flagship promise. Migration should preserve this balance.
