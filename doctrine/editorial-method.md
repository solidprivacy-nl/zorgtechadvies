# Editorial Method

## Purpose

Public content must demonstrate independent judgement without creating false certainty or hidden vendor preference.

## Required evidence separation

Every substantive market/research piece distinguishes:

- **Verified fact** — independently supportable source evidence.
- **Vendor claim** — statement originating from a supplier.
- **ZorgTechAdvies interpretation** — our analysis or judgement.
- **Unknown / not verified** — material point for which evidence is insufficient.

## Permanent transparency box

For market-facing publications include, where relevant:

- publication/update date;
- scope and exclusions;
- selection method;
- criteria used;
- primary source types;
- commercial relationships/conflicts if any;
- uncertainty and unavailable information;
- distinction between facts, claims and interpretation.

## Vendor rule

Default to criteria, fit and trade-offs — not winner rankings.

A conclusion may say that a solution appears stronger for a defined use case when evidence supports it. Avoid unqualified "best supplier" claims.

## Current-anchor rule

Book-derived content must contain a current anchor: regulation, inspection insight, market development, new technology, procurement trend or other relevant present-day signal.

## Original-value gate

Every external piece must contain at least one element that is not generic reproduction:

- original practice insight;
- original framework;
- proprietary data;
- independent synthesis;
- defensible original judgement.

If that element cannot be identified, the piece is not publication-ready.

## Human-review gate

No article, post, newsletter, vendor analysis or sales-facing research asset is published without human review.
