# Doctrine — Zorgtechnologie als zorgkritische keten

## Core principle

Care technology is not an isolated IT product. It becomes care-critical when failures, delays, routing errors, access problems, poor adoption or unclear ownership can affect safety, freedom, continuity or workload.

## Design principles

- safety and continuity first;
- function before technology;
- standardise where possible;
- exceptions only when explicit, explainable and manageable;
- open and future-proof architecture where possible;
- ownership and governance early;
- privacy and security by design;
- adoption and support are part of the solution;
- measure value before scaling;
- make trade-offs explicit rather than hiding compromise.

## Explicit trade-offs

Every analysis should make visible where relevant:

- safety vs freedom;
- speed vs care;
- customisation vs manageability;
- innovation vs predictability;
- local preference vs chain interest.

## Nine-domain lens

1. continuity and safety;
2. governance and chain ownership;
3. infrastructure, building and manageability;
4. VOS/alarm flow and escalation;
5. living circles / wandering prevention / Wzd;
6. cameras, sensors and AI;
7. adoption and change;
8. privacy and security;
9. Proof of Value, decision and controlled scaling.

## Credibility rule

ZorgTechAdvies does not claim neutrality by saying "vendor-neutral" alone. Neutrality must be visible in method, evidence separation, criteria, trade-offs and willingness to state uncertainty.
