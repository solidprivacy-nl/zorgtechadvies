# Voice and Quality

## Voice

Clear, businesslike, sharp, practical and evidence-aware. Avoid hype, generic consultancy language and inflated certainty.

## Writing tests

A public asset should:

- state the real problem, not only the technology;
- speak in the language of board, management, innovation or project leadership;
- remain functional before technical where possible;
- show relevant trade-offs;
- translate abstract value into outputs or decisions;
- make uncertainty visible;
- avoid sounding like vendor marketing;
- end with one logical next step, not multiple competing CTAs.

## Prohibited patterns

Avoid:

- "AI will transform healthcare"-style generic claims;
- unqualified vendor rankings;
- invented sources or unverifiable statistics;
- content written primarily to maximise search volume;
- forced sales CTAs on early-stage awareness pieces;
- synthetic personalisation that reads automated.

## Author model

Use one primary expert voice for regular publishing. The second founder may contribute subject-matter input, review and amplification. Two inconsistent half-voices should not compete for recognition.

## Pre-publication quality gate

Before publication verify:

1. strategic relevance;
2. evidence and source accuracy;
3. original value;
4. consistency with doctrine;
5. customer-phase fit;
6. vendor-neutral framing;
7. useful CTA or intentional absence of CTA;
8. current date/update marker where timeliness matters.
