# Replacement-Wave Research — Feasibility First

## Working idea

Investigate whether public evidence can support a defensible market view of care-technology renewal/reorientation horizons in Dutch VVT and disability care.

Potential public framing:

**De vervangingsgolf 2026–2030 — waar en wanneer heroriëntatie op zorgtechnologie aannemelijk wordt**

Avoid claiming that an organisation *must* replace a system in a given year unless that is explicitly supported.

## Why this matters

If feasible, one research asset can generate:

- proprietary market insight;
- authority content;
- serialised articles/posts;
- better source class D intelligence;
- a multi-year account timing dataset.

## Feasibility sprint

Maximum initial budget: 8–12 hours.

Target sample: roughly 15–20 organisations across VVT/GZ.

For each record try to find:

- organisation;
- system/platform category;
- supplier where public;
- original livegang / major renewal date;
- source and publication date;
- whether the evidence is first-party, supplier claim or independent;
- confidence;
- lifecycle/reorientation indicator, explicitly marked as inference.

## Go criteria

Scale only if:

- sufficient density of traceable dates exists;
- evidence is reproducible;
- the result can be published without overstating certainty;
- the data is useful both editorially and commercially.

## Stop rule

If the initial sample cannot produce a credible dataset, stop. Publish a narrower observation or regional/system-type analysis if useful; do not spend 50+ hours rescuing an unviable premise.
