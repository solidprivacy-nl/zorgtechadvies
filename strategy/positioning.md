# Positioning

## Core position

ZorgTechAdvies helps care organisations make and execute defensible choices in care technology.

We treat care technology not as an isolated IT question but as a **care-critical chain** in which safety, continuity, workability, ownership, privacy, security, adoption and sustainable operations must work together.

## Flagship entry

**KetenScan** is the primary flagship entry when the real assignment is not yet sufficiently clear.

It is designed to create:

- bestuurbaarheid;
- risicoreductie;
- richting;
- een uitvoerbare vervolgstap.

The KetenScan remains productised, priced and visible. It is not mandatory when an organisation is clearly already in another phase.

## External message discipline

Do not position ZorgTechAdvies as a generic advisory firm or as seven equal services.

Externally:

1. one doctrine;
2. one flagship entry point;
3. evidence of expertise;
4. phase-aware next steps.

Internally, the proposition portfolio supports routing.

## Primary audience

- bestuur en directie;
- management;
- innovatie / zorgtechnologie;
- programma- en projectleiding;
- ICT/CIO where relevant.

Primary sectors:

- VVT;
- gehandicaptenzorg.

Netherlands is the active primary market. Flanders is passive/inbound-only until Dutch traction justifies a second jurisdictional motion.

## Positioning tests

Every proposition and public asset must answer:

- for whom is this relevant;
- what problem or decision does it solve;
- why now;
- what concrete output is delivered;
- what is explicitly not included;
- what is the logical next step;
- why is ZorgTechAdvies credible and vendor-neutral here.
