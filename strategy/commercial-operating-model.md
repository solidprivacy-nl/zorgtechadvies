# Commercial Operating Model

## 1. Market model

ZorgTechAdvies operates in a small named B2B market. The operating model therefore prioritises named-account coverage, phase accuracy and qualified conversations over broad reach.

`Addressable` does not mean `sales-worthy`. Direct attention requires a plausible combination of:

- fit;
- signal/timing;
- access;
- phase hypothesis;
- credible value of a ZorgTechAdvies intervention.

## 2. Customer-phase vocabulary

One vocabulary is used across lead intelligence, content, CRM and proposition routing:

1. `PRE_PROBLEM` — no explicit problem definition yet.
2. `PROBLEM_DEFINITION` — assignment/route still open.
3. `PVE_SELECTION` — requirements, procurement or supplier selection.
4. `IMPLEMENTATION` — execution underway.
5. `STALLED_RESET` — trajectory is materially stuck or contested.
6. `POST_GO_LIVE` — tuning, adoption, benefits and assurance.
7. `MATURE_FRAGMENTED` — portfolio fragmentation, standardisation or lifecycle question.

Phase is a hypothesis until verified.

## 3. Proposition routing

- `PROBLEM_DEFINITION` -> KetenScan
- `PVE_SELECTION` -> PvE / functionele uitvraag / leveranciersselectie
- `IMPLEMENTATION` -> implementatie- en ketenregie
- `STALLED_RESET` -> projectreset / second opinion / independent assurance
- `POST_GO_LIVE` -> Proof of Value / adoptie / borging
- `MATURE_FRAGMENTED` -> portfolio- en standaardisatiereview
- `PRE_PROBLEM` -> relationship/nurture; no forced offer

## 4. Sales rule

Every outbound action must be tied to a concrete visible signal or a verified relationship context. First outreach asks for a short exploration or verification, not an assignment.

Do not exploit sensitive incidents or regulator findings as opportunistic hooks. Public supervisory evidence may inform research and risk hypotheses; direct outreach requires an ethical relevance check.

## 5. Content-sales integration

For every meaningful account interaction record:

- phase hypothesis before contact;
- signal/evidence;
- last relevant content shared;
- verification question;
- actual phase after contact;
- next action;
- proposition hypothesis or qualified proposition;
- stated prior awareness of ZorgTechAdvies.

This creates a learning loop between publishing, lead intelligence and sales.

## 6. Capacity rule

Default total market-development budget: 10 hours/week for both founders together unless explicitly changed.

When a live qualified conversation or delivery competes with planned publishing, the live commercial/delivery obligation usually wins. Maintain at least one approved core article in reserve to avoid total publication silence.
