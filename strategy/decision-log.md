# Decision Log

## 2026-08-14 — Repository and deployment

**Decision:** GitHub becomes canonical source of truth for strategy, lead intelligence, website source, content and roadmap. Cloudflare is deployment target.

**Consequence:** manual production-only HTML edits are deprecated after migration.

## 2026-08-14 — KetenScan positioning

**Decision:** KetenScan remains the flagship and primary public entry point when the assignment/route is unclear. It is not a mandatory prerequisite for all services.

**Rejected extreme:** turning the homepage into an equal seven-service catalogue.

## 2026-08-14 — Commercial vocabulary

**Decision:** customer phase becomes the shared vocabulary across content, lead intelligence and sales.

## 2026-08-14 — Lead-state discipline

**Decision:** desk research produces facts, inferences and hypotheses. A proposition hypothesis is not a qualified opportunity until verified.

## 2026-08-14 — NURTURE

**Decision:** NURTURE is a relationship state, not a screening-score status.

Screening status and relationship state must be stored separately.

## 2026-08-14 — Existing adviser / G4

**Decision:** presence of an incumbent adviser is context, never an automatic routing gate. It can even create second-opinion or assurance relevance.

## 2026-08-14 — Editorial cadence

**Decision:** sustainable default is one LinkedIn expertise moment/week and one source-grounded core article/month. Temporary bootstrap acceleration is allowed.

## 2026-08-14 — Newsletter

**Decision:** no separate monthly newsletter production in early year one. Email capture may be enabled from day one. `KetenSignaal` is reserved as a future format.

## 2026-08-14 — Vendor comparisons

**Decision:** favour market interpretation, decision criteria and fit frameworks. Avoid simplistic public rankings or "best vendor" tables.

## 2026-08-14 — AI

**Decision:** AI supports verifiable tasks (triage, extraction, repurposing, consistency). No autonomous external publication or mass-personalised outreach.

## 2026-08-14 — Website evolution

**Decision:** preserve the current KetenScan-led homepage architecture. Add phase awareness, portfolio visibility, knowledge archive and evidence rather than rebuilding the site around the full portfolio.

## 2026-08-14 — Proprietary research

**Decision:** test the proposed replacement-wave study through an 8–12 hour feasibility sprint before committing significant research capacity.
