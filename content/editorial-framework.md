# Editorial Framework

## Objective

Build recognition, trust and qualified demand among a named market of care-sector decision makers without turning ZorgTechAdvies into a content factory.

## Editorial inventory

The book is the evergreen backbone. Each core piece should combine:

`one doctrine chapter + one current anchor + one customer phase + one original insight + one practical instrument + one phase-appropriate next step`

## Five external expertise domains

1. **Bestuurbare zorgtechnologie** — problem definition, governance, ownership and decision-making.
2. **Betrouwbare zorgkritische keten** — VOS, network, building, alarm flow, continuity and manageability.
3. **Vrijheid, toezicht & AI** — Wzd, living circles, cameras, sensors, AI and trust.
4. **Van pilot naar werkende praktijk** — PoV, implementation, adoption, tuning and assurance.
5. **Marktduiding & lifecycle** — market developments, architectures, procurement and lifecycle without simplistic rankings.

## Sustainable cadence

After bootstrap:

- 1 LinkedIn expertise moment/week;
- 1 core website article/month;
- 1 practical instrument every 1–2 months;
- optional flagship proprietary study when evidence feasibility is proven;
- no separate newsletter production until the audience/list justifies it.

## Distribution hierarchy

1. primary expert's personal LinkedIn profile;
2. direct sharing with relevant named accounts/contacts where useful;
3. ZorgTechAdvies website as durable evidence/library;
4. company LinkedIn page as archive/institutional proof, not a second full editorial engine;
5. future email format `KetenSignaal` once list size/engagement justifies it.

## CTA by phase

- `PRE_PROBLEM` -> no offer; follow/read/relationship.
- `PROBLEM_DEFINITION` -> framework/tool, then KetenScan exploration.
- `PVE_SELECTION` -> selection/requirements framework.
- `IMPLEMENTATION` -> explore implementation/chain-direction challenge.
- `STALLED_RESET` -> explore reset/second opinion.
- `POST_GO_LIVE` -> adoption/PoV/assurance check.
- `MATURE_FRAGMENTED` -> portfolio/standardisation exploration.

## Repurposing rule

Research once, publish multiple formats:

`research dossier -> core article -> LinkedIn angles -> visual/instrument -> sales snippet -> future newsletter component`

Repurposed outputs may not introduce new factual claims unless separately sourced and reviewed.
