# Operating Scorecard

## Principle

Measure commercial learning and market penetration, not publication activity for its own sake.

## 1. Named-market reachable coverage

Measure:

- number of in-scope organisations identified;
- number of relevant roles identified in a private/appropriate system;
- percentage of target people with a legitimate reachable route/connection.

A LinkedIn connection is not proof that someone saw a post. Treat it as reachability, not exposure.

## 2. Qualified conversations

Per quarter:

- total qualified conversations;
- customer phase;
- proposition type;
- source class;
- account segment.

## 3. Phase accuracy

For every contacted account:

`phase hypothesis before contact -> actual phase after contact -> correct? yes/no/uncertain`

This is the primary validation signal for the lead model.

## 4. Awareness source

Ask during intake:

`Wat had u van ZorgTechAdvies gezien voordat we spraken?`

Record free-text plus controlled category:

- LinkedIn post;
- website article;
- book/instrument;
- referral;
- direct outreach only;
- other;
- unknown.

## 5. Qualified pipeline/value

Once actual opportunities exist, track:

- opportunity count;
- proposition;
- estimated value band;
- stage;
- win/loss reason.

Do not infer statistically meaningful conversion by topic when n is too small.

## Diagnostic metrics only

Useful for troubleshooting but not north-star metrics:

- impressions/reach;
- likes/comments/shares;
- article views;
- number of published pieces;
- number of accounts in a spreadsheet.

## Capacity economics

Per cycle record:

- research hours;
- content hours;
- outreach/sales hours;
- conversations;
- proposals;
- assignments.

If research quality is high but conversations remain low, test the time allocation before adding more scoring complexity.
