# Proposition Portfolio and Routing

## Principle

**One doctrine, multiple execution forms.**

The public brand is not a menu of unrelated services. The proposition portfolio exists to match the organisation's verified phase and need.

## 1. KetenScan — flagship

**For:** organisations where the real assignment, scope or route is still unclear.

**When it fits:** supplier choice pressure, stalled decision-making, diffuse functional question, conflicting stakeholder views, uncertain innovation scope.

**Includes:** intake, document review, targeted interviews, diagnosis, scenarios/trade-offs, chain risks, decision memo and defensible next step.

**Outcome:** governability, risk reduction, direction and an executable next step.

**Not included by default:** full PvE, procurement execution, implementation management.

## 2. PvE / functional specification / supplier selection

**For:** organisations with a sufficiently clear problem definition entering requirements and selection.

**Outcome:** functional criteria, explicit constraints, evaluation logic and objective selection support.

## 3. Implementation and chain direction

**For:** programmes already executing where cross-domain coordination, dependency management or vendor orchestration is the limiting factor.

**Outcome:** executable governance, dependency control, decision rhythm and delivery direction.

## 4. Project reset / second opinion / independent assurance

**For:** materially stalled, contested or high-risk trajectories.

**Outcome:** independent diagnosis, failure pattern, reset options, risk/decision memo and restart conditions.

## 5. Proof of Value

**For:** organisations testing whether an innovation delivers measurable value and is manageable enough to scale.

**Outcome:** baseline, KPI set, pilot design, success/stop criteria, evidence and go/no-go advice.

## 6. Adoption and assurance / post-go-live optimisation

**For:** recently live environments with alarm load, workarounds, trust, tuning or operationalisation issues.

**Outcome:** tuning rhythm, adoption actions, operational controls and sustainable line ownership.

## 7. Portfolio and standardisation review

**For:** mature but fragmented organisations with multiple local variants, suppliers or lifecycle concerns.

**Outcome:** portfolio view, standardisation logic, exception model, lifecycle priorities and decision roadmap.

## Routing rule

A proposition listed next to an account is always a **proposition hypothesis** until customer phase and need are verified.
